Manifesto

The outer ring is the idea. The inner ring is the people.

CosBioSys is a living answer to one question: where, on this planet, am I already at home? The answer is plural. It stretches across nine world regions, takes six different shapes, and rests on four simple criteria. It is the physical counterpart of EvoBioSys — the digital mesh that carries the work, kept close to its ground.

Every place listed here meets a threshold: continuity, a trusted host, a dependable bed and desk, and a chain of introductions that does not break. The places move slower than the internet. They grow at the pace of love and trust — once, and for life.

Where EvoBioSys does local organizing, those efforts touch ground here. Its Community Hubs — Feldbach in Styria and Integrales Wien — are among the Europa nodes in this network: the places where the digital mesh becomes a doorway you can actually walk through.

The four criteria

What makes a place yours

The places are instances. The criteria are the design.

01

Continuity

A sense of continuity over time. Not a hotel. Not a one-off. Somewhere the arc of visits tells a single story — returnable, recognisable, remembered.

02

A host who would introduce you

A go-to contact person. Someone who would introduce you to the next person, who would introduce you to the next — an unbroken chain of welcome.

03

Somewhere to leave things

The ability to leave some belongings there, or trusted people there, or both. Weight that doesn't need to travel with you means roots that have started to take.

04

A dependable bed & desk

A dependable place to sleep — quiet, warm, for two. A desk where work can continue. The ordinary furniture of a real home, even when you are not the one who built it.

Six kinds of places

The shapes a place can take

Every node in the mesh falls into one of six categories. They are not ranked. A mesh needs all six.

☝

Our family home

The one we are building. Close to a station, close to nature, walkable from a bakery. A garden that meets the sunrise.

An intention held with Kaja: a family home on the axis between Graz and Vienna — Mürzzuschlag, Joglland, and points along the rail corridor between them. Working concept name: Waldinsel.

✭

Family's places

The places the family of origin already holds. Doors that are always unlocked. The first layer of the mesh.

✶

Friends' second homes

The second homes of friends who would host us without coordination. Keys under pots. Beds made just in case.

✿

Communities where friends are members

Co-housing projects, horse communities, retreat cooperatives — places that are not ours, but where a friend's membership is the ticket in.

❖

Retreat locations we co-initiated

Places we helped shape from the beginning. Part-authored. Part of the founding story. Always returnable.

✹

Established retreat locations

Mature places that meet the criteria already. No co-founding needed — only a go-to contact person who holds the door.
