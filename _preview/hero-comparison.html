Version 1

CosBioSys · a holon of EvoBioSys

Rooted here.
Reaching everywhere.

CosBioSys is a guild cultivating regenerative ways of living: cosmo-local in practice, grounded in one place, in kinship with many.

Local

Every regenerative practice starts on the ground someone actually stands on: this soil, these neighbors, this season.

CosBioSys members tend real places first. A guild is not a headquarters, it is many hands doing local work well.

Global

What grows well in one place gets passed on like a cutting from a garden: to sister guilds, into shared commons, through open methods anyone can replant.

CosBioSys stays in kinship with EvoBioSys and other holons doing regenerative work elsewhere, so no place has to learn everything alone.

Rooted locally

Practices grow from real soil, real neighbors, one place at a time.

Connected globally

What one place learns travels the network, and returns changed.

Local roots, global reach: one living guild.
